Trailock — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-25

Trailock does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The app makes no network requests. It does not talk to any server, Trailock's own or anyone else's.
  • Your progress (which levels you've finished) and your settings (board look, dark mode, sound, haptics) are saved only on your device, using local storage. They are never transmitted anywhere and never leave your device.
  • There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no way for us to identify you.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected in the first place.

If you uninstall the app, everything it stored on your device is deleted with it.

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds ads or an in-app purchase — this page will be updated first, and the update will describe exactly what changed and why.
